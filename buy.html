BUY THE OREGON COAST

See the property. Understand the property.

Buying here can mean evaluating far more than bedrooms, baths and a view.

01

Coastal exposure

Salt, wind, moisture and driving rain can affect roofs, siding, decks, fasteners, windows and maintenance cycles.

02

Water + drainage

Topography and how water moves around a property deserve attention — especially on slopes, low areas and sites with significant rainfall.

03

Condos + HOAs

Reserves, assessments, rental restrictions, insurance and shared building maintenance can matter as much as the unit itself.

04

Rental potential

Zoning, permits and local rules are location-specific. “It was rented before” is not the same thing as verified future eligibility.

05

Insurance

Coastal location, condition, flood exposure and building type can affect availability and cost. Get answers early.

06

Future maintenance

A beautiful view can come with an ownership rhythm. Understanding that rhythm before closing is part of making a good decision.
