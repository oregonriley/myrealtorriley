SELL WITH RILEY

Price it with context. Present it with intention.

A coastal property deserves a strategy built around what it actually is — not a checklist copied from somewhere else.

01

POSITION

Know what the buyer is comparing.

Price is only one part of the decision. Condition, location, rental potential, HOA structure, view, access and perceived future work all shape buyer behavior.

02

PREPARE

Spend where it matters.

I want sellers to understand what the market may reward, what it may ignore, and what could create unnecessary questions during inspection.

03

PRESENT

Tell the property's real story.

Photography, description, showing access and listing details should make the property easy to understand without pretending away its realities.

BEFORE WE LIST

Not every house needs the same advice.

Some properties need paint. Some need documentation. Some need a contractor. Some simply need better photography and a price that matches the current market.

My construction background helps me talk through prep practically, and my job as your broker is to keep that work connected to the actual sale — not turn your home into an endless pre-listing project.

THE SELLER PLAN

Clear decisions before the sign goes up.

01Walk the property
